Are there any places that left a strong impression on you? Koharu: Hmm, there’s more than enough, actually… …… Yeah, there were a lot of exciting things, but there are sights I’ve seen without even knowing why. MC: Sights you saw without knowing why? Koharu: On a whim, I joined some indigenous people who made a living by hunting caribou. MC: …The Gwich’in tribe, right? I’ve heard about them from my father. But…… Every time I thought about going home, the starry sky was so beautiful. Looking at the sky every night doesn’t fulfill my goal as a backpacker, to see one new sight every time, and it’s inefficient. But the starry sky was so beautiful, I couldn’t bring myself to go home… It was so beautiful I just had to stay. In the end, after waiting and waiting, when I finally saw the herd– I was so moved. MC: I understand! Me too, more than any remote place… I love HAMA. (About Koharu… I thought he was a shrewd inscrutable person, but maybe we have more in common than I expected.) So, honestly, I want to improve KOBE and JPN. For me, these kinds of feelings are hard to come by.
